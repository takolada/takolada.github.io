Vibration Analysis (Internship at PT PLN Nusantara Power)

PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Indramayu is a company engaged in steam power generation. PLTU Indramayu has a production capacity of 3x330 MW distributed to the Java and Bali regions, especially West Java Province and DKI Jakarta. In carrying out its production process, PLTU Indramayu operates many systems including turbine, boiler, and balance of plant systems. These systems do not always run normally. In the turbine system, there is a machine that often experiences high vibration, namely the generator stator cooling water system pump. The author analyzed the cause of high vibration of the machine. The analysis was carried out based on vibration through the stages of field observation, literature study, data collection, data processing, problem diagnosis, and recommendation preparation. The results of this high vibration cause analysis are that high vibration is caused by looseness and misalignment due to mounting damage on the motor baseplate. Recommendations to overcome the high vibration are to replace the baseplate to prevent repeated misalignment due to mounting deformation, perform alignment, and install epoxy grout to prevent fatigue failure due to undamped vibration.
