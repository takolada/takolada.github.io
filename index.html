Vibration Analysis (Internship at PT PLN Nusantara Power)

Skills: Vibration Analysis, Troubleshooting, Root Cause Analysis, Technical Reporting

During my internship at PT PLN Nusantara Power UP Indramayu, a steam power generation company, I focused on analyzing a recurring issue of high vibration in the generator stator cooling water system pump. This critical machine impacts the power plant's production capacity (3x330 MW). My analysis involved field observation, literature review, data collection, data processing, problem diagnosis, and recommendation formulation. The investigation revealed that the high vibration was caused by looseness and misalignment due to damage on the motor baseplate. To mitigate this, I recommended replacing the baseplate, performing precise alignment, and installing epoxy grout to prevent fatigue failure from undamped vibrations.

Design Optimization of Soft Robot Bionic Hand Manipulator using Supervised Machine Learning

Skills: Soft Robotics, Machine Learning, Design Optimization, CAD, Research

Around 16% of the global population lives with disabilities, with hand amputation being a common cause. Bionic hands offer a solution, but traditional models made from rigid materials lack the flexibility of a human hand. To address this, the Biomechanics Research Team at ITB developed a soft robotic bionic hand using a combination of soft materials, a rigid structure, and strain limiters to mimic natural hand movement.
Achieving a 90-degree range of motion is key to enhancing the hand's functionality. However, optimizing soft robot design is computationally intensive due to its complexity. To reduce this burden, machine learning was used to find optimal designs more efficiently. Algorithms such as linear regression, decision tree, random forest, k-nearest neighbors, and support vector machine (SVM) were tested using simulation-generated data.
The decision tree model improved the index finger's range of motion by 16%, while the SVM model enhanced the thumb and little finger designs by 11% and 21%, respectively, compared to previous designs. Further research is needed to explore additional algorithms and optimization strategies.
